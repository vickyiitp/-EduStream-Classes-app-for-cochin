## 🎓 Highlights

- 🎥 **Organized Subject Modules**: Physics, Chemistry, Math, and Biology syllabi divided into chapter video playlists.
- 📝 **Automated Chapter Tests**: Timed MCQ assessments with instant scorecards.
- 💬 **Doubt Resolution Desk**: Students can flag questions and submit snapshots for teacher clarification.
